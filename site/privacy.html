Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

The short version

Aftr does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads. Your health data is read from Apple Health on your iPhone, used on your iPhone, and never uploaded by Aftr.

A post only leaves your phone when you tap Share and choose where it goes.

What Aftr reads from Apple Health

With your permission, Aftr reads the following from Apple Health to turn your activity into posts. You choose which types to allow, and can change that at any time.

DataUsed for
Workouts, including type, time, duration and energyThe workout post and its stats
Workout routes (GPS)Drawing the route and the map
Heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, VO₂ maxStats you can show on a post
Distance (walking, running, cycling, swimming)Distance, pace and mile markers
Steps and flights climbedThe steps post
Sleep, including sleep stagesThe sleep post and the morning alert
Activity summary (Move, Exercise, Stand)The Today card

Aftr does not write anything to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never sold or shared with third parties.

What Aftr keeps on your iPhone

Aftr does not copy your health data into its own storage. It reads from Apple Health each time it needs to. The only things it saves, on your iPhone only, are:

  • The gym focus you pick for a workout (for example, Push or Legs)
  • Your settings, such as post style, format and whether the lock is on
  • A marker of which workouts it has already told you about, so alerts aren’t repeated

Posts you create are written to a temporary file on your iPhone so they can be shared, and iOS clears them over time. Deleting Aftr deletes everything it saved.

What Aftr never does

  • No account or sign-up, so there is no profile, email or password to store or leak
  • No Aftr servers: nothing you do in the app is sent to us
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising tools inside the app
  • No selling, renting or sharing of any data
  • No storing health data in iCloud or any other cloud service

Sharing and maps

Sharing is always your choice. When you tap Share, Aftr hands the image or video to the iOS share sheet, and you pick the destination, such as Instagram or Messages. From then on, that app’s own privacy policy applies.

Your start and finish stay private. Shared routes leave out the first and last 150 meters, so a post doesn’t reveal where you live. Exported images and videos contain no GPS data.

Map backgrounds come from Apple Maps. When the Map option is on, Aftr asks Apple Maps for an image of the area around your route. That request goes to Apple, not to us, and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. Turn Map off on the share screen and no request is made.

Captions, alerts and the lock

  • Caption ideas are written by Apple Intelligence’s on-device model, on iPhones that support it. Your workout details stay on the phone and are never sent to an AI service.
  • “Ready to post” and good-morning alerts are created on your iPhone when Apple Health records a new workout or night of sleep. No push server is involved.
  • Face ID lock uses Apple’s Face ID system. Aftr never sees or stores your face data; it only learns whether the unlock succeeded.

Your choices

  • Change Health access: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Aftr.
  • Turn alerts on or off: Settings → Notifications → Aftr.
  • Delete everything: delete the app. Your data in Apple Health is not affected.

Children

Aftr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Aftr ever starts collecting data, you will be asked first.
